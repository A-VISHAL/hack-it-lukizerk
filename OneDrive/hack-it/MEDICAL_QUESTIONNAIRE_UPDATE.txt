📋 QUESTIONNAIRE STRUCTURE:

✅ SECTION 1 — General Eye Symptoms (8 Questions)
   1. Eye Pain (None/Mild/Moderate/Severe)
      - Type: Sharp / Burning / Throbbing / Dull ache
   2. Redness (None/Mild/Moderate/Severe)
      - Pattern: Localized / Diffuse
   3. Vision Blur (None/Occasional/Persistent/Sudden onset)
   4. Light Sensitivity (None/Mild/Strong/Extreme)
   5. Tearing/Watering (None/Mild/Excessive)
   6. Itching (None/Mild/Severe)
   7. Foreign Body Sensation (Yes/No)
   8. Dryness (None/Mild/Moderate/Severe)

✅ SECTION 2 — Visual Disturbances (4 Questions)
   1. Floaters (None/Increased recently/Sudden onset CRITICAL)
   2. Flashes of Light (None/Occasional/Frequent)
   3. Double Vision (None/Horizontal/Vertical)
   4. Halos Around Lights (None/Occasional/Persistent)

✅ SECTION 3 — Symptom Duration (Dropdown)
   - Less than 1 day
   - 1-3 days
   - 1 week
   - 2 weeks
   - Chronic (>1 month)

✅ SECTION 4 — Affected Eye (Button Group)
   - Right Eye
   - Left Eye
   - Both Eyes

✅ SECTION 5 — Recent Trauma or Events (Multi-select)
   - Eye injury
   - Chemical exposure
   - Rubbing eye frequently
   - Recent infection or fever
   - Contact lens overuse
   - None

✅ SECTION 6 — Medical History (Critical for Iris Changes)
   
   Systemic Conditions:
   - Diabetes (No/Yes/Pre-diabetic)
   - Hypertension (No/Yes)
   - Thyroid Disease (No/Yes)
   - Autoimmune Disorders (No/Yes)
   
   Previous Eye Surgeries:
   - LASIK / Cataract / Glaucoma / Retinal / Other / None
   
   Family History:
   - Glaucoma / Uveitis / Macular Degeneration / Cataracts / None

✅ SECTION 7 — Medication & Lifestyle
   - Steroid eye drops usage (Yes/No)
   - Contact lens daily use (Yes/No)
   - Smoking history (Never/Former/Current)
   - Known allergies (text input)
   - Screen time per day (<2h / 2-4h / 4-6h / 6-8h / >8h)
   - Long-term medications (text input)

✅ SECTION 8 — Additional Notes
   - Free-text area for detailed observations
